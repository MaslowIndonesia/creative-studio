Story bible

Our Daily Pets began the day a pet parent refused to accept 'just feed it kibble' for a sick companion — a Bekasi cat-mom's fight became a movement of pet parents who refuse to settle.

  • Nadia, the Bekasi cat-mom (core persona (ODP)) — The everyday hero: loves her cats like family, watches every rupiah, and just wants to get it right without being fooled.
    pet pain: picky eating, sensitive stomach · owner pain: budget vs quality, no time to research, needs a trustworthy shortcut
  • The Pet Defender (influencer / affiliate archetype) — A real pet parent turned advocate after their own pet's health scare — now defends pets from bad nutrition and empty marketing.
    pet pain: weak immunity / recurring illness, dull coat + poor digestion · owner pain: guilt + worry when a pet is unwell, distrust of over-claiming brands
  • The Vet Ally (credibility character) — The calm expert voice who cuts through misinformation with plain, honest explanations — never fear-mongers, never over-claims. (Present as a real vet only once one is on record.)
    pet pain: misdiagnosed diet issues, aging / mobility · owner pain: information overload, fear of medical over-claims
  • The Shelter Hero (cause partner) — A shelter/rescue keeper whose rescues transform on real nutrition — every recovery is a second chance. (Claim a partnership only once one really exists.)
    pet pain: abandoned / malnourished rescues, recovery after neglect · owner pain: wanting to do genuine good, trusting where money actually goes
